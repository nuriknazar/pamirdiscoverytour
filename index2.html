The Experience

PRIVATE JOURNEY
THROUGH THE
ROOF OF THE WORLD

Private adventure tours between Dushanbe and Osh — one of the world's highest mountain roads. Experience the legendary Pamir Highway, breathtaking mountain landscapes, remote villages, and unforgettable cultural encounters on a private 4×4 expedition through the heart of Central Asia.

Travel the legendary Pamir Highway, one of the world's highest mountain roads — scenic drives along the Panj River, the historic Wakhan Valley, and views of Lenin Peak before crossing into Kyrgyzstan.

— The Route Highlights
🏔

Legendary Pamir Highway

One of the world's highest mountain roads — a bucket-list drive across the roof of Central Asia.

🌊

Panj River & Afghan Border

Scenic drives along the Panj River with views across into Afghanistan.

🏛

Wakhan Valley & Fortresses

Visit the historic Wakhan Valley and ancient Silk Road fortresses.

🏔

Lenin Peak Views

High-altitude Eastern Pamir landscapes with views of Lenin Peak before crossing into Kyrgyzstan.
